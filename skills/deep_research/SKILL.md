# Deep Research Skill

When conducting deep research on a topic:
1. Deconstruct the user's research topic into 2-3 specific inquiry questions.
2. Query knowledge sources and search tools for each facet.
3. Compare perspectives, discard low-quality or irrelevant snippets.
4. Synthesize findings into:
   - Executive Summary
   - Key Facts & Data Points
   - Trade-offs / Analysis
   - Recommended Next Steps or Sources
